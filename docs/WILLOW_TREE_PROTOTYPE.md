## Result and limits

6,119 triangles, including 2,591 repaired woody triangles and 3,528 foliage triangles. 84 hanging groups, each with three crossed curved ribbons of seven segments. Nine bones: fixed root plus eight phase-offset crown controls. Two meshes/materials, maximum two influences per vertex. 1024-square foliage atlas with four variants; bark source 1024, QA runtime bark 512. Texture data is uploaded to local EditableImages once, not changed per frame.

The existing S-trunk/root silhouette is retained. Voxel reconstruction repaired inward/intersecting branch faces; duplicated triangles were removed. Source coarse triangular foliage is replaced. The crown remains broad in front and narrower in side view, following the supplied mesh's depth. Its upper contour still has some pointed clusters, and low-poly wood facets remain visible close up. These are explicit art-review items, not approved final art. Crossed ribbons overlap intentionally; no coplanar duplicate faces remain. Full 360-degree moving-camera review on a phone is still needed.

The Blender FBX round trip confirms UVs, rig and weights. Measured preview foliage displacement is 0.6695 studs; trunk displacement is zero. The eight-second baked loop has a 0.00425-stud seam due to FBX curve quantization. Runtime wind uses continuous functions instead of that baked loop.
